Classic Banana Bread

Ingredients:

  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1.5 cups all-purpose flour

Instructions: Mix the ingredients together in a large bowl, pour into a loaf pan, and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 1 hour.

Easy Tomato Pasta

Ingredients:

  • 200g pasta
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Fresh basil leaves

Instructions: Cook pasta. Sauté garlic in olive oil, add tomatoes and simmer. Toss pasta with the sauce and garnish with basil.
